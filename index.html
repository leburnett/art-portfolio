Artist Portfolio

I work in oil pastel and cut relief blocks, drawn to the point where a colour stops behaving and a surface starts showing its grain. Most of what I make begins outdoors and is finished slowly at a table indoors. This is a record of the work, printed and drawn, as it accumulates.
