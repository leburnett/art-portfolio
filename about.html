About

Hello! 👋


My name is Laura and I'm a linocut relief printmaker and oil pastel artist currently based in London.

For as long as I can remember I have loved creating. I take a lot of inspiration from nature and natural forms as well as interesting and vibrant colour combinations.

I find both mediums that I work with meditive in different ways.

I enjoy focussing on the minutae and the detail in the linocut prints, and allowing myself to think more abstractly and appreciate the tactile nature of oil pastel work.

Thank you for visiting my site.
