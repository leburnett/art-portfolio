About

Hello! 👋


Welcome to my personal portfolio page — a place where I can digitally archive and share my recent creations.

Recently, I have mostly worked on nature-inspired linocut prints and abstract oil pastel pieces. I enjoy focussing on the detailed minutae in the linocut prints and the viscerally tactile aspect of using oil pastels.

Outside of art, I am a trained research scientist and, unsurprisingly for someone so affected by what I see around me, I have spent over a decade researching how brains use and interpret visual information.

Thank you for visiting my site and please get in touch if you have any comments or questions.
